FAQ

Common questions about Ferrari rental in Zurich.

What is included in the Ferrari rental experience

Personal support, a full handover and briefing, and concierge assistance during your booking.

How do I request a date

Use the request form or WhatsApp. We confirm availability and terms personally.

Do I pay in advance

Yes. Payment is always in advance. Your booking is confirmed after payment and written acceptance of the terms.

Is a security deposit required

Yes. A refundable deposit is required. CHF 1,500 for drivers aged 25 or above, CHF 3,000 otherwise. It is refunded after the return check.

What documents do I need

A valid driving licence and a valid ID or passport. We may request additional details for insurance.

Can tourists or international visitors rent

Yes, if your licence is valid for driving in Switzerland and you meet our requirements.

Where is pickup and do you offer delivery in Zurich

Pickup is near Zurich. Delivery or alternative pickup is available on request, with transparent fees shared during confirmation.

How many kilometres are included

Each package includes a stated kilometre allowance. Extra kilometres are charged as confirmed for your booking.

Can I add a second driver

Yes, on request. The second driver must meet the same requirements.

What is your cancellation policy

It depends on timing and availability. We confirm the exact cancellation and rescheduling terms in writing before payment.
