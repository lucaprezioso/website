Questions before you plan

Ferrari experience FAQ

Can every Ferrari experience be personalized?

Yes. The standard rental packages provide the duration and kilometre framework, while timing, handover, route suggestions, presentation and occasion-specific details can be discussed individually.

Can you create a tailored wedding or proposal offer?

Yes. Send the venue, schedule, desired photo window and whether the Ferrari is mainly for arrival, driving or photographs. We will reply with a proposal based on the actual logistics.

Can the Ferrari be delivered to a hotel or event location?

Delivery may be possible depending on the date, duration, access and location. Include the exact address in the request so it can be priced and confirmed properly.

Are mountain-drive suggestions available?

Yes, when the package and season allow it. We recommend realistic Swiss routes based on available time, traffic patterns and road conditions, but the final route remains the driver’s responsibility.

Can the Ferrari be booked only for a photoshoot or corporate event?

Yes. Static use, short movements and longer hosted arrangements can be considered. We need the concept, location, timing and access requirements before confirming.

Can I buy the experience as a gift voucher?

Yes. A voucher can be created for a package or agreed value and personalized with the recipient’s name and message.
