Curated routes

Start with the climb to the resort, then choose a pass day when open. In summer, the region offers long, rhythmic roads and wide views.

  • Chur to Davos for a smooth approach before the mountain roads
  • In season: Flüela Pass toward the Lower Engadin for long elevation
  • In season: Albula Pass to Bergün for a classic Graubünden loop
  • Engadin day trip via Flüela when conditions allow for wide open scenery

Practical notes

  • Valid driving licence and an ID document required
  • A short walk through of the car at handover, then you are ready
  • Fuel policy and kilometres depend on the chosen package
  • Return location and timing are confirmed in writing

Davos: high altitude, long rhythm

Davos gives the drive a high Alpine rhythm from the first kilometres. Depending on season, the route can point toward Flüela, Landwasser, Lenzerheide or the wider Graubünden network, with scenery that feels very different from Zurich.

For Davos, we usually recommend planning more time than the minimum. Elevation, weather and tourist traffic can change quickly, and a Ferrari experience is better when the day has enough space for route adjustments.
