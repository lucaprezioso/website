Curated routes

Include the approach from Chur. When the passes are open, Flüela and Albula can form a longer loop through the Engadin.

  • Chur via Landquart and Klosters to Davos
  • Flüela Pass from Davos to Susch and the Lower Engadin
  • Albula via La Punt, Bergün and the Landwasser valley for a longer loop
  • A valley drive around Chur instead of the high passes when conditions are unsuitable

Practical notes

  • A short walk through of the car at handover, then you are ready
  • Fuel policy and kilometres depend on the chosen package

A day between Davos and the Engadin

Flüela Pass leads from Davos to Susch. A longer loop can continue through the Engadin and La Punt, over Albula Pass and through Bergün, returning along the Landwasser valley. Allow a full day with room for traffic, visibility and stops. We assess whether it suits your package using the complete distance from and back to the agreed handover point.

A day drive or a weekend

12 hours allows more time for a pass drive; 24 or 48 hours can support a stay in Davos with a separate driving day. Mileage limits apply to the entire rental, including the approach and return from Chur. You book and pay for accommodation and meals separately. The high passes are seasonal, and your route needs to suit the current weather.

Frequently asked questions

How much does sports car rental in Davos cost?

The Ferrari 458 Italia starts at CHF 440 for 3 hours with unlimited kilometres. Handover: CHF 290 per agreed pickup or return. The refundable deposit is paid separately: CHF 1,500 for drivers aged 25 or over and CHF 3,000 for drivers aged 21 to 24. We confirm the package, meeting point and return arrangements in writing before booking.

Is a personal briefing included?

Yes. We explain the controls, parking and the particular features of the Ferrari 458 Italia. Preparation includes a short drive together; your booked rental time starts afterwards.

Is insurance included?

Yes. Insurance is included. In normal insured cases, there is no insurance excess for the renter. An excess or additional liability may apply in cases such as negligence, misuse of the vehicle or a breach of the rental agreement.

Is the Ferrari collected directly in Davos?

Collection for Davos is normally in Chur. The fee is CHF 290 per agreed pickup or return. Another meeting point requires personal confirmation; we agree its feasibility and fee before booking.
