Our quiet alternative to central Zurich

Nänikon is not a showroom address chosen for appearance; it is the practical base of Luxury Obsession near Uster and Greifensee. The advantage is simple: the handover can feel calmer, and the first kilometres can lead quickly toward open roads instead of inner-city traffic.

For guests from Zurich, the Zurich Oberland or eastern Switzerland, this location often makes short packages more efficient. It is also a good starting point for Greifensee, Zürcher Oberland, Walensee and pre Alpine routes.
