Ferrari routes from Lucerne: lake, Entlebuch and Alpine passes

Lake Lucerne: Weggis and Vitznau

One possible drive leads from Lucerne via Küssnacht to Weggis and on to Vitznau. Allow time for a lakeside stop and your return journey. For a short booking, we adapt the turning point to traffic, stops and your return time.

Entlebuch: country roads and scenery

Head via Wolhusen towards Entlebuch and Schüpfheim for country roads and the region’s rolling landscape. Choose the length of the outing to suit your package; adding a mountain pass needs extra time.

Brünig and Klausen: destinations for longer drives

The approach towards Brünig runs via Sarnen, Giswil and Lungern. Plan Klausen separately via Altdorf towards the pass. Both suit a longer booking with time for stops and the return journey. We check weather, road conditions and the seasonal opening of Klausen Pass.

Frequently asked questions

How much does sports car rental in Lucerne cost?

The Ferrari 458 Italia starts at CHF 440 for 3 hours with unlimited kilometres. Handover in Lucerne: CHF 140 per agreed pickup or return. The refundable deposit is paid separately: CHF 1,500 for drivers aged 25 or over and CHF 3,000 for drivers aged 21 to 24. We confirm the package, meeting point and return arrangements in writing before booking.

What is the handover fee for Lucerne?

Handover in Lucerne costs CHF 140 per agreed pickup or return. We confirm the meeting point, time and return plan in writing before booking. Alternatively, collection from our Nänikon base is included in every package.

Is a personal briefing included?

Yes. We explain the controls, parking and the particular features of the Ferrari 458 Italia. Preparation includes a short drive together; your booked rental time starts afterwards.

Is insurance included?

Yes. Insurance is included. In normal insured cases, there is no insurance excess for the renter. An excess or additional liability may apply in cases such as negligence, misuse of the vehicle or a breach of the rental agreement.

Is a deposit required?

Yes. The refundable security deposit is CHF 1,500 for drivers aged 25 or over and CHF 3,000 for drivers aged 21 to 24. It is paid separately from the rental price and refunded after the rental, provided there are no outstanding charges or damages.

Can I book the Ferrari for a wedding or an event?

Yes. We can coordinate timing and a discreet handover so the car fits naturally into the day.

Is a three-hour drive from Lucerne enough?

Three hours can suit a manageable drive by Lake Lucerne, with the turning point chosen around traffic and planned stops. The briefing and short accompanied drive take place before your booked rental time starts. For Brünig, Klausen or an extended Entlebuch tour, we recommend a longer package.

Which routes can I plan with the Ferrari from Lucerne?

Options include Weggis and Vitznau by Lake Lucerne, Wolhusen and Schüpfheim in Entlebuch, or Sarnen and Lungern towards Brünig. Klausen Pass is a separate destination via Altdorf for a longer driving day. Include weather, pass opening, stops, your return time and package kilometres in the plan.
