Frequently asked questions

How does pickup in Aargau work?

After your request, we confirm availability, package and meeting point in writing. On the day, we meet at the agreed location and give you a calm introduction to the car, so you can start with confidence. Pickup fee: CHF 140.

Is the briefing included?

Yes. Every booking includes a complete briefing on the controls, parking and smooth driving of the Ferrari 458 Italia.

Is insurance included?

Yes. Insurance is included. The deductible and conditions are stated in the booking confirmation.

Is a deposit required?

Yes. A refundable deposit is required. The amount depends on the package and driver profile.

Can I book the Ferrari for a wedding or event?

Yes. We can coordinate timing and discreet delivery, so the car fits into the day without unnecessary stress.
