FAQs

How does pickup in Zurich work?

After your request we confirm availability, package and pickup details in writing. On the day, we meet at the agreed location and do a calm walkthrough so you can drive with confidence.

Is a briefing included?

Yes. Every booking includes a thorough briefing on controls, parking, and how to drive the 458 smoothly.

Is insurance included?

Yes. Insurance is included. The customer excess and conditions are stated in your booking confirmation.

Do I need to pay a deposit?

A refundable security deposit applies. The amount depends on the selected package and the driver profile.

Can I book the car for a wedding or a special day?

Yes. We can coordinate timing and a discreet handover so the car fits your day without stress. Request availability and details.
