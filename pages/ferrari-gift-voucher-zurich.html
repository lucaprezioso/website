What to expect on a first Ferrari drive

Before you give the voucher

How long is the voucher valid?

Vouchers are generally valid for two years. The applicable validity and expiry date are confirmed when the voucher is issued.

Do I need to choose the driving date now?

No. The recipient can arrange the date later with us, subject to availability and the voucher’s validity.

Can it be a surprise?

Yes. We can coordinate the voucher discreetly with you. The recipient’s eligibility and documents still need to be confirmed before the drive.

Can a booked drive be postponed?

Postponement arranged in advance is possible at no extra cost. Contact us before the booked date so we can agree a new available date and confirm it in writing.

Can I ask for a refund if I change my mind?

A refund requested simply because you change your mind incurs a fee. We confirm the applicable fee and voucher conditions before purchase.

What if the weather is unsuitable?

Contact us in advance to discuss the conditions and arrange a postponement where needed. Do not assume a booking changes automatically; we confirm the new date together.

Who provides the deposit?

A refundable deposit is required separately before the rental. Tell us whether you want to arrange it yourself or leave it to the recipient, so the gift does not create an unexpected obligation.

Can I give a full week or a monetary amount?

Yes. The one-week package is CHF 4,480 with 1,300 km. Alternatively, choose an agreed voucher value; we confirm any balance due when the rental package is selected.

Can I receive the voucher quickly?

A digital voucher can be sent by email. Tell us your gift deadline and we will confirm what is possible. Physical cards and postage are arranged separately.
