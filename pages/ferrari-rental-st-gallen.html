Sports car rental in eastern Switzerland

St. Gallen is our agreed handover point for a drive through eastern Switzerland. Appenzell, Toggenburg and the Rhine valley offer different routes for a half-day or full-day outing. Other handover locations can be discussed on request.

Appenzell and Säntis: drive to Schwägalp

One possible route leads from St. Gallen via Appenzell and Urnäsch to Schwägalp at the foot of Säntis. Drive to Schwägalp for views of the mountain; the summit itself is not accessible by car. Allow enough time for a return through Toggenburg and check the weather and road conditions beforehand. Include stops, your return time and your package’s kilometre allowance in the plan.

Sports car rental around Lake Constance

For a Lake Constance drive, start in St. Gallen and head to the Swiss shore, for example Rorschach, Arbon or Romanshorn. Combine a Ferrari drive with a stop by the water. If you are visiting from Germany or Austria, you can also arrange a handover with us in Switzerland.

Frequently asked questions

How much does sports car rental in St. Gallen cost?

The Ferrari 458 Italia starts at CHF 440 for 3 hours with unlimited kilometres. Handover in St. Gallen: CHF 140 per agreed pickup or return. The refundable deposit is paid separately: CHF 1,500 for drivers aged 25 or over and CHF 3,000 for drivers aged 21 to 24. We confirm the package, meeting point and return arrangements in writing before booking.

Is a personal briefing included?

Yes. We explain the controls, parking and the particular features of the Ferrari 458 Italia. Preparation includes a short drive together; your booked rental time starts afterwards.

Is insurance included?

Yes. Insurance is included. In normal insured cases, there is no insurance excess for the renter. An excess or additional liability may apply in cases such as negligence, misuse of the vehicle or a breach of the rental agreement.

Is a deposit required?

Yes. The refundable security deposit is CHF 1,500 for drivers aged 25 or over and CHF 3,000 for drivers aged 21 to 24. It is paid separately from the rental price and refunded after the rental, provided there are no outstanding charges or damages.

Can I book the Ferrari for a wedding or event?

Yes. We can coordinate timing and discreet delivery, so the car fits into the day without unnecessary stress.

Can I arrange sports car handover in St Gallen or elsewhere in eastern Switzerland?

Yes. Agreed handover in St. Gallen costs CHF 140 per pickup or return. We consider other meeting points in eastern Switzerland or around Lake Constance on request and confirm the location, time and fees before booking. Collection from Nänikon is included.

Which routes suit a Ferrari drive from St. Gallen?

Depending on your time, package and the weather, consider Appenzell and Urnäsch, Schwägalp at the foot of Säntis, or the Swiss Lake Constance shore at Rorschach, Arbon and Romanshorn. Drive to Schwägalp for views of the mountain; the summit itself is not accessible by car. We help you plan realistic routes, stops and distances.

Can I drive into Germany or Austria around Lake Constance?

Only with our prior written approval. Without this approval, the Ferrari may only be used within Switzerland. Visitors from Germany or Austria can arrange a handover in Switzerland; this does not automatically permit driving across the border.
