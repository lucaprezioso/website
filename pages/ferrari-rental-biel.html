Frequently asked questions

How does Ferrari pickup in Biel work?

Pickup in Biel is available with a delivery fee of CHF 290. After your request, we confirm availability, package and handover details in writing. On the day, we meet at the agreed location, complete a calm briefing and hand over the Ferrari 458 Italia.

Is the briefing included?

Yes. Every booking includes a complete briefing on the controls, parking and smooth handling of the Ferrari 458 Italia.

Is insurance included?

Yes. Insurance is included. The deductible and relevant conditions are stated in the booking confirmation.

Is a deposit required?

Yes. A refundable deposit is required. The amount depends on the package and driver profile.

Can I book the Ferrari for a wedding or event?

Yes. We can coordinate timing and a discreet handover so the car fits into the day without stress.
