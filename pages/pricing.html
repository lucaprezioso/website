How to compare Ferrari rental prices

When renting a Ferrari in Zurich, the price should always be considered together with the duration, mileage package, deposit, pickup point and planned route. A cheaper-looking offer may be less suitable if the time window is too short or the kilometres do not match the drive you have in mind.

Our rates are structured around typical experiences: short impression, half day, full day, weekend and week. Before booking, we confirm in writing what is included, which kilometres apply and which additional costs arise only for special requests.

How it works

  1. 1. Request
    Send your preferred date, time, and rate. We reply personally with availability and a clear confirmation.
  2. 2. Confirmation and payment
    Once confirmed, payment is made in advance to secure the booking. You receive a written confirmation with the agreed details.
  3. 3. Refundable deposit
    A refundable deposit is provided at or before pickup. It is held as security and returned after the drive if the car is returned undamaged.
  4. 4. Meet at the pickup location
    We meet at the agreed location. Identity and licence are verified, and you receive a short briefing on the car and basic rules.
  5. 5. The drive
    Enjoy your time with the car within the agreed duration and mileage. We stay reachable should anything come up.
  6. 6. Return
    Return the car at the agreed location and time. A short walk around and confirmation of fuel and mileage closes the handover.
  7. 7. Deposit refund
    If there is no damage, the deposit is reimbursed immediately or within the agreed window after inspection.

Times and locations are agreed individually. Every request is confirmed personally before payment.

Questions about rental prices and the deposit

How much does it cost to rent the Ferrari for a day?

For a day trip, 12 hours starts at CHF 940 with 300 km. To keep the Ferrari overnight, a full 24 hours costs CHF 1,340 with 200 km, CHF 1,590 with 500 km or CHF 1,990 with unlimited kilometres.

How much does a Ferrari weekend cost?

The Weekend Escape package covers 48 hours and costs CHF 1,990 with a total of 500 km or CHF 2,790 with a total of 800 km. We agree the exact pickup and return times when confirming your booking.

How many kilometres are included, and what do extra kilometres cost?

The mileage allowance covers the entire rental period. The 3-hour Brief Escape includes unlimited kilometres. Unlimited mileage options are also available for 6, 12 and 24 hours. For packages with a mileage limit, additional kilometres cost CHF 5 each.

How much is the deposit, and is it included in the rental price?

The refundable security deposit is CHF 1,500 for drivers aged 25 or over and CHF 3,000 for drivers aged 21 to 24. It is paid separately from the rental price and is refunded after the rental, provided there are no outstanding charges or damages. In normal insured cases, there is no insurance excess for the renter. An excess or additional liability may apply in cases such as negligence, misuse of the vehicle or a breach of the rental agreement.
