Frequently asked questions

How does Ferrari pickup in Bern work?

After your request, we confirm availability, package, pickup fee and exact meeting point in writing. On the day itself, we meet at the agreed location, complete a calm briefing and hand over the Ferrari 458 Italia.

What is the pickup fee for Bern?

Pickup in Bern is available with a delivery fee of CHF 290. The exact meeting point and return plan are confirmed with your booking.

Is the briefing included?

Yes. Every booking includes a complete briefing on controls, parking and smooth driving of the Ferrari 458 Italia.

Is insurance included?

Yes. Insurance is included. The deductible and the relevant conditions are stated in the booking confirmation.

Is a deposit required?

Yes. A refundable deposit is required. The amount depends on the package and the driver profile.

Can I book the Ferrari for a wedding or an event?

Yes. We can coordinate timing and a discreet handover so the car fits naturally into the day.
