Curated routes

For a dramatic first hour, climb straight into the gorge. In season, the high passes turn a half day into a classic Alpine loop.

  • Schöllenen Gorge up to Andermatt for a dramatic warm up
  • Oberalp Pass for wide views and smooth climbs
  • In season: Furka and Grimsel loop for high Alpine scenery
  • Gotthard Tremola old road when open for historic cobbles and hairpins

Practical notes

  • Valid driving licence and an ID document required
  • A short walk through of the car at handover, then you are ready
  • Fuel policy and kilometres depend on the chosen package
  • Return location and timing are confirmed in writing

Why Andermatt works for a Ferrari drive

Andermatt is one of the most dramatic bases for a Ferrari 458 Italia because the drive starts close to real elevation rather than city traffic. The surrounding Gotthard region gives the experience a precise Alpine character: tighter sections, changing light, and scenery that makes the naturally aspirated V8 feel even more special.

Because pass roads depend strongly on season, weather and traffic, we treat Andermatt bookings as curated drives rather than generic handovers. For this location, a 12h, 24h or weekend package usually gives the right margin for route planning, photo stops and a relaxed return.
