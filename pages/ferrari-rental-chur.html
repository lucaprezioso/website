Curated routes

Chur offers lower valley roads as well as longer pass drives. Allow a full day for the high passes when the season and weather are suitable.

  • Rhine valley for a drive on lower roads
  • Lenzerheide and Tiefencastel for a central Graubünden route
  • Domleschg, Thusis and Viamala as a valley route
  • Via Bergün and the Albula Pass to La Punt, or via Julier into the Engadin, subject to pass status

Practical notes

  • Valid driving licence and an ID document required
  • A short walk through of the car at handover, then you are ready
  • Fuel policy and kilometres depend on the chosen package
  • Return location and timing are confirmed in writing

Chur as a base for Graubünden and Davos

After handover, stay in the Rhine valley or travel via Lenzerheide and Tiefencastel towards Bergün and the Albula Pass. Julier and Albula offer different routes into the Engadin; they are planning alternatives, not compulsory stops on a short rental. A Davos trip normally starts with collection in Chur. The onward drive to your hotel and the return count towards rental time and package mileage.

Time for the passes and a weekend

For a longer pass drive, consider 12 hours, 24 hours or the 48-hour Weekend Escape, depending on the complete route. The 12-hour option starts at CHF 940 with 300 km; weekend packages offer 500 or 800 km. Include the approach, return, photo stops and time for changing weather. If the high passes are closed, plan a lower route rather than an unavailable loop.

How much does sports car rental in Chur cost?

Is a personal briefing included?

Yes. We explain the controls, parking and the particular features of the Ferrari 458 Italia. Preparation includes a short drive together; your booked rental time starts afterwards.

Is insurance included?

Yes. Insurance is included. In normal insured cases, there is no insurance excess for the renter. An excess or additional liability may apply in cases such as negligence, misuse of the vehicle or a breach of the rental agreement.

Where do I collect the Ferrari for a trip to Davos?

Davos pickups are normally arranged in Chur. The fee is CHF 290 per agreed pickup or return. We confirm the meeting point and times in writing. The onward drive to Davos and the return count towards rental time and booked mileage.
