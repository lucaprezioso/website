  • Domleschg to Thusis and Viamala for dramatic scenery

Why Chur is a strong starting point

Chur works especially well as a gateway into Graubünden. From here the drive can stay elegant and flowing in the Rhine valley or become more alpine toward Lenzerheide, Viamala, Julier or Albula when conditions allow.

This makes Chur a good choice for guests who want more landscape than a city drive but still need a practical handover. The Ferrari 458 Italia feels at home on this mix of open valleys, clean bends and mountain approaches.
