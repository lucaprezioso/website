Ferrari pickup near Zurich, Zurich Airport and across Switzerland

Luxury Obsession is based in Nänikon, close to Uster and Zurich. This location gives guests a discreet starting point outside the city while keeping Zurich, Zurich Airport and the main Swiss road network within easy reach.

For guests staying in Zurich city, arriving through Zurich Airport or planning a scenic drive from central Switzerland, the handover can be arranged around the rental package and the expected route. The Ferrari 458 Italia is prepared before every experience and handed over with a clear explanation of the car, the rental terms and the return procedure.

Pickup in the Zurich area

Our Nänikon host point is included with every rate. Zurich Airport pickup costs CHF 50 for the 3-hour Brief Escape and is included with packages of 6 hours or more in 2026. Pickup in Zurich and Winterthur costs CHF 80 for the 3-hour and 6-hour packages and is included with packages of 12 hours or more in 2026.

Regional and extended pickup

For Zug, Lucerne, St. Gallen and Aargau, the standard pickup fee is CHF 140. For Bern, Biel, Basel, Solothurn and Chur, the standard pickup fee is CHF 290. Davos is normally handled through Chur, while Andermatt is normally handled through Altdorf UR.

Every pickup fee is shown before booking. There are no unclear delivery surcharges hidden at the end of the process.
