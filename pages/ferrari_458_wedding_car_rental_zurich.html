Practical planning tips for a Ferrari wedding car rental in Switzerland

  • Book early for peak dates. Popular weekends go first, especially in spring and summer.
  • Build in time buffer. A wedding schedule always benefits from extra minutes.
  • Keep decorations removable. Ribbons are fine when agreed in advance. Adhesives and anything that can mark the paint should be avoided.
  • Keep the route simple. One arrival, one photo stop, or one short drive usually works better than trying to do too much.
  • Choose the right driver. Self drive means the designated driver must be eligible, comfortable, and completely alcohol free.
