A red Ferrari 458 Italia for your photoshoot in Zurich

Picture a portrait framed by the open driver's door, the red silhouette against a plain wall or a close-up of the carbon steering wheel. Our Rosso Corsa Ferrari 458 Italia is available for private portraits, editorial shoots and filming in and around Zurich.

When you’re in the picture

You might have a shot in mind, or simply want a portrait of yourself with the Ferrari. We work with experienced photographers and filmmakers and have agreed special terms with them for shoots we arrange together.

Our gallery focuses on the car. Our partners have also photographed and filmed people on several shoots. If you rarely find yourself in front of a camera, they can help you settle into the driver’s seat or find a relaxed pose beside the car.

Tell us what you’d like to take away from the shoot. We can advise you on a package and check which photographer or filmmaker would suit it. Please get in touch well in advance, ideally before fixing a date, so we can coordinate the team, the Ferrari and the location.

Planning your photoshoot or film shoot

With the Ferrari parked

For portraits, fashion photography and interior details, the Ferrari can stay in one spot. A photo of the entrance and the space you have in mind helps us check ramp clearance and room to open the doors. If you’re still choosing a backdrop, explore our photo locations around Zurich and Uster.

With the Ferrari in motion

Would you like the 458 to roll into frame or pull up outside a building? Tell us about the shot, the location and who would be driving. We’ll check what’s possible there and which rental conditions apply.

Questions about your Ferrari shoot

Can I bring my own photographer or film crew?

Yes. You can hire the Ferrari separately and work with your own team. We’ll agree on the location, access and timing with you.

When should I enquire if I’d like to work with your team?

Get in touch while you’re planning the shoot, before you commit to a date. That gives us time to check availability with the right photographer or filmmaker and reserve a suitable slot for the car.

Where can the shoot take place?

You’re welcome to suggest a location in Zurich or the surrounding area. The Ferrari sits low, so we’ll check access to the spot and confirm that the venue allows the shoot.

What would you like to shoot?

Send us your preferred date, location and time window. A few lines about the shots you have in mind, whether the car needs to move and whether you’re bringing a team will help us put together a proposal. For vehicle hire options, see our rental packages and prices.
