Arrival, luggage and requirements for visitors

We arrange these details before your flight so you can take over the Ferrari without rushing.

Handover after landing

Send us your flight number, scheduled arrival time and a phone number we can reach you on after landing. We agree the handover time allowing for baggage reclaim and entry formalities. Your landing time is therefore not automatically your handover time.

Let us know as early as possible about flight delays or delays at baggage reclaim. We agree a revised handover window subject to availability and confirm any changes personally. Allow time for the vehicle briefing and a short drive together, too.

Small bags and hotel luggage transfers

The Ferrari 458 Italia has a relatively spacious front luggage compartment for a two-seater. Small bags may fit; we check this with you before booking using the number and dimensions of your bags.

For larger suitcases or additional luggage, we can arrange separate delivery to your hotel on request for an extra fee. We confirm the destination, amount of luggage, timing and additional price beforehand. Luggage delivery is not included in free airport handover.

Documents, minimum age and deposit

The minimum age is 21. Every driver must bring a valid original driving licence and an original ID document and must be approved by us. If you are travelling with a foreign driving licence, confirm its acceptance and any additional documents needed with us before booking.

The refundable deposit is CHF 1,500 for drivers aged 25 or over and CHF 3,000 for drivers aged 21 to 24, paid separately from the rental price. Rental payment is made in advance; we confirm the booking, payment deadline and any further requirements in writing. Travel outside Switzerland requires our prior written approval. Frequently asked questions

Where exactly does airport handover take place?

By prior arrangement, at the valet meeting point or in an airport car park. We confirm the exact meeting point and access instructions in writing beforehand, including the level and parking space where applicable. The customer pays the parking ticket.

What happens if my flight is delayed?

Share your flight number and arrival time and let us know about delays as early as possible. We personally agree a revised handover window subject to availability. We allow for baggage reclaim and entry formalities when arranging the meeting.

Will my luggage fit in the Ferrari?

Small bags may fit in the relatively spacious front luggage compartment. Send us the number and dimensions of your bags so we can check. Larger or additional suitcases can be delivered to your hotel on request for an extra fee agreed in advance.

Is airport handover really free from 6 hours?

Yes. Zurich Airport handover is included with packages of 6 hours or more in 2026. For the 3-hour package, it costs CHF 50 per agreed pickup or return. Parking tickets and optional luggage delivery are charged separately.

What are the requirements for visitors from abroad?

The minimum age is 21. All approved drivers must present a valid original driving licence and an original ID document. We check foreign driving licences and any additional documents before booking. The deposit is CHF 1,500 for drivers aged 25 or over, or CHF 3,000 for drivers aged 21 to 24.

How much does sports car rental at Zurich Airport cost?

Is a personal briefing included?

Yes. We explain the controls, parking and the particular features of the Ferrari 458 Italia. Preparation includes a short drive together; your booked rental time starts afterwards.

Is insurance included?

Yes. Insurance is included. In normal insured cases, there is no insurance excess for the renter. An excess or additional liability may apply in cases such as negligence, misuse of the vehicle or a breach of the rental agreement.

Is a deposit required?

Yes. The refundable security deposit is CHF 1,500 for drivers aged 25 or over and CHF 3,000 for drivers aged 21 to 24. It is paid separately from the rental price and refunded after the rental, provided there are no outstanding charges or damages.

Can I book the Ferrari for a wedding or event?

Yes. We can coordinate timing and discreet delivery, so the car fits into the day without unnecessary stress.
