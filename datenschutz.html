Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how we process personal data when you visit luxuryobsession.ch, contact us, or request and book a rental. It applies to our website and to communication via email, phone, and WhatsApp.

Personal data we process

  • Website usage and technical data: IP address, browser and device information, date and time of access, referring page, and logs generated by hosting and security services.
  • Inquiries and booking requests: name, email, phone number, preferred dates, package, pickup and delivery preferences, and any message content you send us.
  • Contract and compliance data: information needed to conclude and execute the rental contract (for example identity and driving licence details, age verification, address, signatures, payment and deposit records, and correspondence about fines or incidents).
  • Vehicle handover documentation: photos and checklists taken at pickup and return (for example condition of the vehicle, mileage, fuel level, time and location).

Purposes and legal basis

We process personal data to answer inquiries, prepare and confirm bookings, conclude and perform the rental contract, provide customer support, comply with legal obligations, and protect our rights and assets. Where applicable, processing is based on contract performance, legitimate interests, and legal obligations. If the GDPR applies, consent may be required for certain optional technologies.

Vehicle security and tracking

For theft prevention and asset protection, the rental vehicle may be equipped with security devices such as a GPS tracker and, where applicable, a dash camera. If such devices are in use, we process the related data only for security, incident handling, and enforcement of contractual and legal claims, and we share it only where necessary (for example with insurers, legal counsel, or authorities).

Cookies and similar technologies

  • Essential storage: we store your language preference in local storage (key: lo_lang) so the site remembers your selected language.
  • Third party content: embedded or external services can set cookies or similar identifiers and may receive technical data (for example IP address).

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics, a web analytics service provided by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies to analyze how visitors use our website.

The information generated about your use of this website (including IP address, device and browser information, pages visited, and time of access) may be transmitted to and stored on servers outside Switzerland and the European Economic Area, including the United States.

We use Google Analytics to understand how our website is used, to improve our services, and to evaluate the effectiveness of our online presence. The data is processed in aggregated form and is not used for automated decision making.

You can prevent the storage of cookies by adjusting your browser settings. You may also use browser extensions to block Google Analytics.

We do not use marketing or advertising cookies. If we introduce additional optional technologies in the future, we will update this policy accordingly.

You can delete cookies in your browser settings and block third party cookies. Blocking cookies may affect certain features.

Third party services and transfers abroad

Depending on how you use the site and which services are loaded, your personal data may be processed by third parties, potentially outside Switzerland:

  • Google Fonts: fonts are loaded from Google servers.
  • Google Maps: map embeds can transmit technical data and may set cookies.
  • WhatsApp: if you contact us via WhatsApp, processing is governed by WhatsApp and Meta.
  • Hosting and security: our hosting and security providers may process technical logs to operate and protect the website.

Where cross border transfers occur, we take measures required by applicable law, such as contractual safeguards and risk assessments.

Retention

We store inquiry and booking related communication for as long as needed to handle your request and to comply with legal and accounting requirements. Technical logs are retained according to provider settings and security needs. We delete or anonymize data when it is no longer required.

Your rights

Depending on applicable law (Swiss FADP and, where applicable, GDPR), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal data. To exercise your rights, please contact us using the details above.

Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our services or legal requirements. The current version is published on this page.
