What we collect

Purpose and legal basis

We process personal data to answer inquiries, to prepare and confirm bookings, to provide customer support, and to protect the security and integrity of the website. Where applicable, processing is based on contract performance, legitimate interests, and legal obligations. If the GDPR applies, consent may be required for certain optional technologies.

Cookies and similar technologies

We aim to keep the website lightweight and do not intentionally use marketing or analytics cookies at this time.

  • Essential storage: we may store your language preference in local storage (key: lo_lang) so the site remembers your selected language.
  • Third party content: embedded content or external services may set cookies or similar identifiers and may receive technical data (for example IP address). See the section below.

You can usually delete cookies in your browser settings and block third party cookies. Blocking cookies may affect certain features.

Third party services

  • Google Fonts: fonts are loaded from Google servers, which can result in technical data being transmitted.
  • Google Maps: the map embed can transmit technical data and may set cookies by Google.
  • WhatsApp link: if you contact us via WhatsApp, data processing is governed by WhatsApp and Meta.

Retention

We store inquiry and booking related communication for as long as needed to handle your request and to comply with legal and accounting requirements. Technical logs are usually retained according to the hosting provider settings.

Your rights

You may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal data, depending on applicable law (Swiss FADP and, where applicable, GDPR). Please contact us to exercise your rights.

Security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal data. However, no method of transmission over the internet is fully secure.
