The experience in motion

The Ferrari 458 Italia, without filters

The 458 Italia is central to our philosophy because it combines modern usability with the character of a naturally aspirated Ferrari V8. Its response, sound and precision turn even a familiar road into a memorable occasion.

As Ferrari’s final naturally aspirated mid-engine V8 berlinetta before the turbocharged era, it occupies a distinctive place in the marque’s modern history. It feels refined when you need it to and intensely mechanical when the road opens up.

A selective approach

Why we choose quality over volume

Luxury Obsession is built around selection rather than volume. Every vehicle must offer distinctive character, excellent condition and a clear reason to be part of the experience. Today, the Ferrari 458 Italia embodies that standard, and every booking is prepared around the client and occasion.

That focus also means honest advice. We confirm whether the requested duration, mileage and pickup plan make sense before anything is finalised, rather than simply accepting every request.

Frequently asked questions

Which Ferrari is currently available to rent?
Luxury Obsession currently offers a Ferrari 458 Italia in Rosso Corsa with a carbon-fibre interior. Every vehicle is selected and prepared to a consistently high standard.
Where can the Ferrari be collected?
Collection is available from our base in Nänikon near Zurich. Pickup options are also available for Zurich Airport, Zurich, Winterthur and selected other locations by prior arrangement.
How is a booking confirmed?
After receiving your preferred date, duration and pickup plan, we verify availability and driver eligibility. The booking becomes confirmed once the rental agreement is signed and the agreed payment has been received.
Is every rental personally managed?
Yes. Each request is reviewed individually, and the handover includes a detailed introduction to the vehicle and a short familiarisation drive.
