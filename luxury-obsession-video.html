The company in motion

An exceptional Ferrari. Direct service. A carefully prepared experience.

Luxury Obsession is an independent Ferrari rental company based in Nänikon near Zurich. Every enquiry, preparation and handover is managed personally, with clear written terms and direct support before, during and after the drive.

The car at the centre of the experience is a Rosso Corsa Ferrari 458 Italia: a naturally aspirated 4.5-litre V8 with 570 PS, a 9,000 rpm redline and a seven-speed dual-clutch transmission. Our selective approach ensures that the vehicle and every client handover receive close attention.

About Luxury Obsession

Luxury Obsession by Anna Loi offers personally hosted Ferrari 458 Italia rentals from the Zurich area. The service is designed for enthusiasts, visitors, gifts, weddings and special occasions where the quality of the handover matters as much as the car itself.

Collection is available from Nänikon, with arranged pickup options including Zurich Airport, Zurich, Winterthur and selected locations across Switzerland. Availability is confirmed individually for every request.
